Gratitude Cards

Psychology Academic Project

A psychology-based academic activity centered on expressing gratitude and appreciation through handmade cards prepared and presented to university faculty and staff.

  • TypeAcademic Project
  • SubjectPsychology
  • ActivityGratitude & Appreciation
  • StatusConducted

About the Activity

The Gratitude Cards project was conducted as part of Psychology coursework and focused on turning appreciation into a simple, tangible action. Handmade cards containing gratitude and appreciation messages were prepared and presented to university faculty and staff.

The activity connected a psychology-related theme with a practical interaction rather than limiting the project to a classroom presentation.

Purpose & Approach

01

Express Appreciation

Create a tangible way to acknowledge and appreciate people within the university environment.

02

Make It Personal

Use handmade cards and individual messages rather than a generic digital message.

03

Turn the Concept Into Action

Take the psychology-related classroom activity into a real interpersonal setting through preparation and distribution.

What We Did

Planning → Card Preparation → Personal Messages → Distribution → Reflection

  • Prepared multiple handmade gratitude cards
  • Used different card and envelope designs
  • Added appreciation-oriented messages
  • Organized the cards for distribution
  • Presented them to university faculty/staff
  • Documented the activity through photographs

Reflection

The project demonstrated how a small act of appreciation can be turned into a meaningful interpersonal activity. The handmade format made the interaction more personal and allowed the project to move beyond theory into a real university setting.

The activity required coordination, preparation and thoughtful presentation while keeping the central idea simple: intentionally expressing gratitude.
